METHOD DESCRIPTIONS:
--------------------
Method 1: Regular Cosine
  - Flatten all frames into single vector
  - Compute cosine similarity once
  - Fast but ignores temporal structure

Method 2: Frame-wise Cosine  
  - Compare frame i with frame i (no alignment)
  - Average similarity across all frames
  - Simple but assumes synchronized videos

Method 3: DTW-Aligned Filtered Frame-wise (RECOMMENDED)
  - Use DTW to find optimal frame alignment
  - Filter out nearly identical pairs (>99% similarity)
  - Calculate frame-wise cosine on remaining pairs
  - Best accuracy by handling temporal variations
